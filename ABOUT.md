# Sa-Ra-L — About Us

> *Disciplined, evidence-driven algorithmic trading for Indian markets.*
> *Complexity in the engine. Simplicity in the cockpit.*

---

## Who we are

**Sa-Ra-L** is a real-money algorithmic options-trading platform for the Indian
markets — NIFTY, SENSEX and USD/INR — built and run with a Chartered Accountant's
discipline rather than a gambler's instinct.

It is not a single "magic" algorithm. It is a **portfolio of independent strategies**,
each with its own logic, its own risk limits, its own instruments, and its own
track record — all operating under **one unified control system** that lets a single
person see everything, prove everything, and act on everything from one screen.

The name *Sa-Ra-L* carries our philosophy: **saral** — to make the genuinely
complex *simple to operate*. Markets are noisy and hard; the cockpit should be calm,
clear, and honest. Behind that calm sits serious machinery — multi-timeframe analysis,
deep historical backtests, regime detection, and named strategies drawn from the
*astras* of Indian lore (Brahmastra, Pashupatastra) — precision instruments, used
sparingly, with respect for their power.

---

## Our Mission

To run real capital in the market **only where the evidence earns it**, by:

1. **Proving before deploying.** No strategy touches real money until it has a deep
   backtest, validated readiness (data, signals, live ticks), and a paper-traded
   shakedown. We trust verified numbers, not stories.
2. **Protecting capital first.** A strict **1:4 capital rule** keeps no more than 20%
   of the book deployed at any time; the rest stays in reserve. Every strategy has a
   hard daily loss cap and an automatic square-off.
3. **Making real-money actions deliberate.** Going live is never one click — it takes
   an armed token and a typed confirmation, with a global STOP that halts everything
   instantly.
4. **Staying honest about what we know.** Modelled premiums are labelled as modelled.
   Short backtests are flagged as short. A losing strategy is called a loser and put
   back on paper. We would rather be uncomfortable and right than comfortable and wrong.
5. **Keeping one source of truth.** Every strategy, backtest, daily analysis, and
   readiness check lives in one dashboard, so decisions are made on a complete picture,
   not scattered notes.

## What we do

- **Trade a diversified portfolio of options strategies** — expiry-day scalpers,
  intraday momentum and mean-reversion, gap fades, black-swan/tail catchers, a
  currency-futures trend system, and rare-release asymmetric setups.
- **Backtest deep and honestly** — real intraday history back to ~2015 via Zerodha
  Kite, multi-year structural backtests, and clear caveats on every number.
- **Operate from one cockpit** — a unified web dashboard for live control, multi-
  timeframe charts with Bollinger Bands, forward-impact projections, pre-market
  analysis, per-strategy readiness, and live capital allocation.
- **Run with guard-rails** — server-side live-order guards, per-strategy and global
  kill switches, and a pre-open GO/NO-GO self-check.

---

## What we want to do next

- **Earn each strategy its capital** — promote only proven performers to live, keep
  the rest on paper until they prove out, and retire what doesn't work.
- **Tighten the evidence loop** — model real costs (brokerage, STT, slippage) so
  backtest P&L converges on what we'd actually book.
- **Deepen the edges** — refine signal quality, add real-volume futures underlyings,
  and let the recorders confirm the rare, high-conviction setups before risking capital.
- **Compound with patience** — small, consistent monthly gains, reinvested, on a book
  that is always more in reserve than in the market.
